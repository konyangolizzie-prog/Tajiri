About Tajiri POS

A local-first retail workspace for sales, inventory, customers, reports and cash management — built for Kenyan shops and businesses.

⌂Multi-storeManage branches, stores and market stalls from one place.
⌁M-Pesa readySTK push via Daraja, paybill, till and split payments.
⚿Role-based accessAdmin, manager and cashier permissions built in.
✧AI insightsReorder suggestions and daily summaries for owners.
